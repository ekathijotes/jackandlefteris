Dinner & celebrations

Lita

A short walk from the town hall, on a quiet street behind Marylebone High Street. Mediterranean cooking over an open fire, in a room built to feel like somebody’s house — which is rather the point. Lita is short for abuelita: grandmother.

The kitchen

Everything comes off an open grill in the middle of the room. The cooking is Mediterranean in spirit and mostly British in its ingredients — Cornish fish, Hereford beef, Lake District lamb — and the plates are built to be shared. Kostas Papathanasiou, who came from Frantzén in Stockholm, runs it.

A note on the menu below

Lita changes its menu with the seasons, so none of this will survive until April 2027. What follows is the list as it stands in November 2026 — a fair picture of how they cook, and a starting point for us rather than a promise. We will settle the actual dishes, wines and cocktails with them closer to the day.
